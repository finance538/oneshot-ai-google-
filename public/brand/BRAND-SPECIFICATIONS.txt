========================================================
oneShot - Official Brand & Logo Identity Specifications
========================================================

1. Brand Unification & Concept:
-------------------------------
The "oneShot" identity fuses two primary visual metaphors:
• The Precision Shutter: 6-blade mechanical camera iris aperture, representing photography, cinematography, and optical perfection.
• The Bullseye Target: Concentric circular reticle with 4 cardinal tick notches and center focal point, representing precision, accuracy, and "taking the perfect shot".

2. Brand Colors:
----------------
• Precision Orange (Accent / "one" / Target Reticle):
  HEX: #FF5500
  RGB: rgb(255, 85, 0)
  CMYK: 0% / 67% / 100% / 0%

• Deep Titanium Obsidian (Dark Blades / Light "Shot"):
  HEX: #12141A
  RGB: rgb(18, 20, 26)

• Studio Ceramic White (Light Blades / Dark "Shot"):
  HEX: #FFFFFF
  RGB: rgb(255, 255, 255)

• Deep Charcoal Dark Canvas:
  HEX: #0A0B0E

3. Typography:
--------------
• "one" (lowercase): Precision Orange, Bold Sans-Serif
• "Shot" (Capitalized 'S'): White in Dark Mode / Charcoal Black in Light Mode
• Recommended Font Family: Outfit Bold / Montserrat / Inter Bold

4. Usage Guidelines:
--------------------
• Minimum clear space equal to 25% of emblem radius around the logo.
• All PNG files in this package feature 100% transparent backgrounds.
• For print and large format billboards, always use the .svg vector files.
