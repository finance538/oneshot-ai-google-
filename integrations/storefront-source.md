# Public storefront source

The public website at `https://1shotcam.com/` was reviewed on October 4, 2026. Its published content supplied the home-page layout, typography, imagery, 48 product descriptions and prices, four media packages, Google Ads package, and seven company and policy pages. The catalog includes products in automation, documents and training, AI, websites, branding, social media, media production, and marketing. Public product scopes, deliverables, exclusions, revision limits and billing cycles were retained. Counts reflect the actual 48 published records, rather than the higher category counts shown in the source metadata.

The supplied `https://github.com/finance538/oneShot-admin` repository and its contents endpoint returned HTTP 404. The original repository, private files, paid document deliverables, customer records and unpublished assets were not imported. The downloadable service briefs describe the public product scope; they are not the purchased document products themselves. An accessible source repository or archive is needed to import any additional files.

Six working public photographs were saved locally under `public/images/` so the page does not depend on remote image availability. The broken payment-terminal image was replaced with a code-rendered illustration. Styling uses scoped vanilla CSS and preserves the existing payment application. The public company and policy routes are `/about`, `/vision`, `/privacy`, `/refund`, `/terms`, `/support`, and `/accounts`.

Service requests use the published WhatsApp and support email channels. A request includes the service title, SKU and published price, with an explicit explanation that no payment has occurred. This is a request-and-confirm flow; a direct public catalog checkout was not added. The original site's unrelated authentication backend, payment endpoint and illustrative SDK were not copied. Sign-in uses the existing local Identity flow and payment integration examples point to the existing merchant workspace. Gateway credentials, payment records, functions and applied migrations were not changed.

Validation covered frontend and backend TypeScript, all 48 catalog items, pagination, combined filtering and search, product pages, inquiry dialogs, service-brief downloads, seven policy routes, English/Arabic direction, theme switching, code copying, merchant sign-in, and responsive layouts at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels. No build command or live payment was run.
